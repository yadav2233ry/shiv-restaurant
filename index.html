HERITAGE & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Rooted in Jaunpur’s Tradition

Nestled in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh, Shiv Restaurant was born from a timeless culinary philosophy: food is not merely sustenance, but a sacred celebration of warmth, fellowship, and ancestral recipes.

From hand-pounded cumin and roasted whole red chilies to simmering golden dal finished with smoking desi ghee, every item on our menu is prepared with unwavering devotion to authenticity. We take pride in serving fresh, unhurried meals that evoke the comfort of traditional North Indian family feasts.
